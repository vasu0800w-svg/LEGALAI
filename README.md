✦ ABOUT CASEMATE

Casemate is an AI-powered legal technology product built for India's legal ecosystem.

Legal professionals deal with extensive documentation, complex case papers and time-consuming drafting processes.

Casemate aims to simplify these workflows through intelligent AI assistance, helping legal professionals spend less time on repetitive documentation and more time on meaningful legal work.

Our goal is simple:

Make legal work smarter, more efficient and more accessible through Artificial Intelligence.

---

⚡ WHAT CASEMATE OFFERS

⚖️ Intelligent Case Analysis

AI-assisted understanding of uploaded legal documents and relevant case information.

✍️ Legal Drafting

Assistance with preparing structured legal first drafts based on user instructions.

📄 Document Understanding

Simplifying the process of reviewing and working with legal documents.

📑 Template-Based Drafting

Supporting workflows that use existing legal document templates.

🌐 Multilingual Assistance

English and Hindi legal assistance workflows.

🧠 Matter-Based Context

Keeping relevant case information organized around the legal matter.
