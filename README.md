◈ 01 — THE VISION

Legal professionals spend significant time reviewing case papers, understanding facts, researching legal issues and preparing legal documents.

Casemate aims to make this process smarter, faster and more organized.

Casemate is an AI legal copilot built to assist advocates and other legal professionals with case-document analysis and legal drafting.

Users can provide case papers, relevant instructions and existing document templates to prepare structured first drafts for professional review.

«Technology should empower legal professionals, not replace their judgment.»

◈ 08 — RESPONSIBLE AI

Casemate is designed as an assistance tool, not a replacement for legal professionals.

- AI-generated drafts require human verification.
- Names, dates, facts, citations and legal provisions must be checked.
- Users remain responsible for reviewing documents before use.
- Casemate does not independently file documents in court.
- AI output should not be treated as guaranteed legal advice or an authoritative judicial determination.

AI assists. Legal professionals decide.
